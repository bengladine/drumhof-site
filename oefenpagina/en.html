Your own practice page

Every Drumhof student gets their own practice page. After the lesson I put your homework there: the beats we played, what to practise and what to watch out for. You just open it on your phone, no app or account needed.

How a week goes

In the lesson we play the new exercises together. Right after, your homework is online. At home you put on a beat, play along and start slow. Once it works at that tempo, you tick it off and go a step faster. Every day you practise, you press "I practised today". In the next lesson we look at how far you got.

Why like this?

Practising at home is hard when you're not sure what to do or how it should sound. With one clear goal per exercise, like "play it 4x clean at 100 bpm", you know when you've got it. And because you can listen to everything, you don't accidentally practise it wrong.

For parents

The page shows what your kid has to practise this week and how many days they've practised. Handy for asking how it's going, even if you can't read drum notation yourself.

Practical

  • Works on any phone, tablet or computer.
  • You can add the page to your home screen, then it opens like an app.
  • Only your first name is on the page. What you tick off stays on your own device.
