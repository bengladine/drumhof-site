Welcome to Drumhof

Looking for drum lessons in Ghent? At Drumhof, I teach both beginners and intermediate drummers. I'm Ben, an experienced drum teacher based in Ghent and passionate musician.

From holding drumsticks for the first time to deepening your core skills, I’ll help you sound like a rock or metal drummer. Expect to learn accents, speed, double bass drum, fills, and your favorite songs. Lessons will be on electric and acoustic kits.

I currently have room for new students. Want to try first? Book a trial lesson for only €20.

Frequently asked questions

I've never played drums before. Can I just start?

Absolutely. Plenty of my students started from zero. We begin with the basics and build up at your own pace.

Do I need a drum kit at home?

Not to get started. Lessons take place on my kit. At home, a pair of sticks and a practice pad will take you a long way at first. If you want your own kit later on, I'm happy to help you figure out what suits you.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes. There's no fixed minimum age: if you can reach the pedals and you're keen, we can get going. Not sure? A trial lesson is the easiest way to find out.

Can I learn a specific song?

Please do. Bring your favourite song and we'll work towards it. It's often the most fun way to pick up new techniques.

How do I pay?

Cash or bank transfer, whatever's easiest for you.
